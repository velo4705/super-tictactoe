How to Play Super Tic-Tac-Toe

🧩

The Board

The game consists of a large 3x3 main board, where each cell contains a smaller 3x3 mini-board.

🎯

Objective

To win, you must win three mini-boards in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on the main board.

⚔️

Mini-Boards

Play in any cell that is not already won or full. Win a mini-board by getting three marks in a row within its grid.

🧠

Strategy

Placing a mark claims that spot on the mini-board. Try to build lines on the main board while blocking the computer's tactics!
